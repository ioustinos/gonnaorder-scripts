📅 Schedule Assigner

GonnaOrder Scripts

Upload a weekly menu (dish external ID + active dates) and assign each GonnaOrder dish to the store schedule whose dates match exactly. No exact match → error, nothing written.

🔒 Credentials are sent over HTTPS to our serverless function, used once for a session token, never stored. Your email is remembered in this browser; your password is not.
Date-based schedules in this store
Only schedules made purely of specific dates (DATE_INCLUSIVE) are matchable. Day-of-week schedules are listed greyed out and never matched.
Extra columns like Day, Category, Dish (EL) are used for display only. A dish appearing on several dates = several rows.
Matching preview
🛡 Every write echoes the dish's entire current record and changes only the schedule (and visibility, if ticked). After each save the dish is re-read and diffed field by field — if anything else changed, the row is marked failed and shown to you. Added after the 2026-08-11 incident where a hand-written payload silently cleared 31 dishes' price descriptions.
